# Daily Circle

Trigger only when the user starts a daily review or begins recounting the day. Adapt tone to the situation. Gather a concise day summary, energy, completed work, work not completed and its reason, learning, open loops, and tomorrow's focus. Ask follow-ups in small batches and do not turn the session into therapy or a productivity interrogation.

Search for today's daily note. Preview and apply one routed daily write. Link canonical project notes instead of duplicating their task lists. Record unproductive days too; they are evidence for weekly patterns.
