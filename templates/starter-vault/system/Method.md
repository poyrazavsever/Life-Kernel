The user chooses how planning works during onboarding. Agents follow this note instead of imposing a method. Until onboarding is complete, leave this blank and offer the options.

## Chosen approach

Examples to offer: themed days, time blocks, a short daily top-three, a weekly outcomes list, or a lighter mix. Record the choice and the reason.
